Studio stage

One line. The whole screen.

Open the Studio stage from the lyrics toolbar and the current line takes over the window, coloured by the cover.

  • Album edge glow. The cover's two strongest colours wrap the edges. Music Sync pulses with the sung word, Idle breathes slowly, Steady holds still, Off keeps it clean — thickness and glow are sliders.
  • Follows the queue. When the next song starts, the stage swaps song, artwork and lyrics instead of keeping the old ones.
  • No stale motion. Reduce Motion desaturates the movement without losing the word timing.
