## LyricalStudio

- Project: https://github.com/arijit-debug/LyricalStudio
- Author: Arijit Naskar
- Copyright (c) 2026 LyricalStudio contributors

The Lyrical Studio animation family in
`lib/features/lyrics/lyrics_studio_styles.dart` adapts the Stack, Slide, Zoom,
Letters, Scatter, Flow, Graph and automatic per-line style selection from
LyricalStudio's `src/templates`.

## theaestheticslyrics

- Project: https://github.com/darshanseenivasakumar/theaestheticslyrics
- Author: darshanseenivasakumar
- Copyright (c) 2026 darshanseenivasakumar

`lib/features/lyrics/lyrics_studio_styles.dart` adapt the layout and motion of
theaestheticslyrics' renderer styles.
